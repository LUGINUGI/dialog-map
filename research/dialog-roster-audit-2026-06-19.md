# Dialog roster audit

Research date: 2026-06-19

## Bottom line

The viral 113-name artifact is a **website “Directory List,” not a clean membership roll**. WIRED later reported that Dialog separately tracks paying members, retreat registrants, guests, speakers, prospects, and historical attendees. Of 192 current dossiers WIRED reviewed, 130 were tagged as members; the unreleased 2026 registration list contains 222 people, including 87 first-time attendees.

The fullest defensible public reconstruction currently consists of:

- 113 people in the exposed public website directory.
- 17 additional named people surfaced in reporting on the separate 2026 registration/dossier data.
- Raffi Grinberg, publicly identified as Dialog’s executive director, who was not in the 113-name directory.
- A separate, older set of advertised or confirmed retreat participants in archived 2013 and 2022 invitations. These should not be presented as current members.

The full 222-person 2026 registration list has not been published by a reliable source. Any online claim to reproduce all 222 should therefore be treated skeptically unless it supplies the underlying records.

## Evidence classes

- **Founder/operator:** a documented organizational role.
- **Active member:** the leaked data explicitly used that label.
- **2026 registrant:** named in reporting on the separate 222-person registration list.
- **Current dossier/prospect:** appears in current vetting data, but membership or attendance is not established.
- **Confirmed attendee/guest/speaker:** the person or reliable reporting confirms the narrower role.
- **Advertised participant:** named in an invitation as expected or confirmed for a retreat; this does not prove completed attendance.
- **Directory entry only:** appears in the 113-name website directory, with no stronger public evidence located.

## What was added, removed, or changed

Wes Moore, Jared Polis, Jim Himes, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Sophia Bush, and Josh Brolin all require labels narrower than “member.” The strongest explicit newly public membership label is Lisa Gevelber’s “Active Member.”

## Recommended data model for the website

Do not use a boolean `isMember`. Use:

```ts
type DialogStatus =
  | "founder"
  | "operator"
  | "active-member"
  | "member-unspecified"
  | "2026-registrant"
  | "confirmed-attendee"
  | "guest-or-speaker"
  | "prospect-or-first-timer"
  | "advertised-participant"
  | "directory-entry-only"
  | "denied-or-disputed";
```

Every status should include a date, source URL, and short caveat. This prevents the map from turning proximity into a false claim of membership.
